# Privacy Policy — GridCore Poster

Last updated: 2026-08-22

GridCore Poster is a personal content-publishing tool. It publishes posts and reads
engagement data (replies, insights) for Threads and Instagram accounts owned and
operated by the app owner only.

## Data we collect
- Access tokens for the owner's own social accounts.
- Public engagement data (replies, like counts, view counts) on the owner's own posts.

We do not collect, store, or process personal data of any third party. We do not
sell or share any data.

## Data deletion
This app stores no third-party user data, so there is nothing to delete. If you
believe this app holds data about you, contact the owner via GitHub
(https://github.com/gojc85-ui) and any such data will be deleted within 30 days.
